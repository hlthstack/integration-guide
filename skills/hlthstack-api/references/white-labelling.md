# White-labelling

Communications to your members (employees of the companies you serve) can go out under your
identity instead of hlthstack's.

> [!NOTE]
> hlthstack sets this up for you; there is no API call to make. See
> [how to set it up](#4-how-to-set-it-up).

## 1. Options

| Option | What changes |
|---|---|
| White-labelled email | Every email to your members goes out under your white-labelled identity instead of hlthstack's. |
| White-labelled WhatsApp and SMS | WhatsApp and SMS messages follow the same white-labelled setup. Available together with white-labelled email. |

## 2. Modes

| Mode | Channels carrying your branding |
|---|---|
| Email only | Email |
| Email, WhatsApp and SMS | Email, WhatsApp and SMS |

## 3. What you can configure

| Element | What it controls |
|---|---|
| App name | The app or platform name shown to members in communications, in place of hlthstack. |
| Sender name | The sender name members see on emails and messages. |
| Logo | Your logo in communications. |

## 4. How to set it up

1. Tell hlthstack through [hlthstack.io](https://www.hlthstack.io/) (or your hlthstack contact)
   which mode you want and for which companies.
2. Send the app name, sender name and logo to use.
3. hlthstack enables it for those companies and confirms when it is live.

**See also:** [product communications](product-communications.md) for what members receive
and when; the [member app embed guide](pwa.md) to theme the
embedded member app itself.
